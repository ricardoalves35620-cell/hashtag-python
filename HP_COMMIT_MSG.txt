pwa: a day of fixes was sitting in a cache

Reported from the app: phase 8's exam still shows "# Construa your dashboard:" — half
Portuguese, half English, and the exact defect reported earlier. The repository has said
"# Monte seu painel:" since 2026-07-29, commit 47b5a7e, NINETEEN commits ago. The
translation was never wrong. It never arrived.

`registerType: 'prompt'` in vite.config.ts is correct and was chosen for a real reason:
with `autoUpdate` a new worker activates under a running tab, cleanupOutdatedCaches
deletes the chunks that tab still holds, the next lazy import 404s, and the learner gets
"Algo interrompeu a aula" mid-lesson. That was reported too.

But 'prompt' only means the new worker WAITS. Something has to tell it to take over, and
nothing did — `registerSW` was never called anywhere in the app. So a build reached a
learner only when every tab and every installed window was closed, which on an installed
app means swiping it out of the app switcher rather than backgrounding it. Nobody does
that, so nothing shipped.

This is the half that was missing: notice the waiting worker, say so, and hand over when
the learner chooses. A prompt rather than an automatic reload, because taking over
mid-exercise throws away whatever is in the editor.

I ranked this fourth of four. It should have been first: it made every other fix in this
session invisible, including the ones I verified end to end in a browser I control. The
verification was real and the delivery was not, which is a distinction worth keeping —
"passes in the app" and "reaches the learner" are two claims, and I had only earned one.
