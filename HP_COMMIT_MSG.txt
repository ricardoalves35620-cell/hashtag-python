audit: the leak detector was reading code between two strings as a string

`db.append({"id": len(db)+1, "client": client, "pages": pages})` has three string
literals, all too short to report. The global regex skipped "id" for being under
three characters and resumed from its CLOSING quote, so it paired that quote with
the opening quote of the next key and reported `": client,"` as printed prose — in
four exercises, pointing at code that was correct.

Replaced with a scan that tracks quote state per line. That also stopped the
detector skipping every literal that followed a short one, so the honest count went
UP before it came down: 152 reported, 160 actually there, 145 after the two pinned
contract strings in phases 13 and 14 were re-excluded. Those two had been invisible
to the regex and are `raise ValueError("negative price")` — values the grader pins,
so translating them breaks the exercises.

Phases 0-20 remain at 0. The remaining 145 are phases 21-68.
